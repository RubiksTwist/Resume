Operations platform for a dog-training business

The Dog Institute

I built the customer and staff platform that replaced Pike13 at The Dog Institute of Williamsburg, a Brooklyn dog-training business. It carried over nine years of customer history, made Square the payment system, and cut card fees from 14.6% of sales in September 2026 to about 3%.

Customer site

Product Surface

Problem

The Dog Institute ran its customers, packages, bookings and card payments on Pike13 and its bundled card processor. The processor kept getting more expensive. Its statements show the effective rate rising from 7.19% of card sales in August 2025 to 16.08% in October 2026.

Leaving Pike13 was not a simple export. Customers had prepaid packages and visit balances, and the business had nine years of history in the system. All of it had to carry over intact.

What I Built

I worked with the founder and the five-person team to map how they look up customers, sell packages and take bookings. Then I built the customer portal and the staff tools around those routines, with analog backups alongside the digital flows. The platform runs on PostgreSQL and Netlify Functions.

Square is the payment authority. Customers buy packages online through Square checkout, and staff sell at the front desk on Square. The platform keeps the package-and-visit ledger. It ties each Square payment, refund and order to the customer's purchases, remaining visits, deductions and history, so staff get an auditable view of every balance.

Payments and Ledger

Migration

Pike13 held nine years of history, from 2017 to 2026. The migration consolidated more than 239,000 source records into 2,180 customer records and carried over their history, packages, balances and visit deductions. When the records could not say for certain who a claim belonged to, the platform sent it to a staff review queue instead of guessing.

Pike13 was retired on November 22, 2026. Its imported history stays in the platform as a read-only record.

Fees, Checked Against Statements

In September 2026 card processing cost 14.6% of sales on the old processor. It runs about 3% on Square. At that month's volume, that is about $6,900 a month less. Square's measured rate on the institute's card payments from January to November 2026 was 2.98%.

Before trusting those figures, I checked the history they rest on. 99 months of processor statements, from August 2018 to October 2026, match Pike13's sales records to within 0.07%.

Design Decisions

Square Takes the Card

Every card payment, online or in person, goes through Square. Card data stays with Square and never enters the platform.

One Ledger for Every Channel

Online purchases, front-desk sales, bookings and staff check-ins all write to the same ledger, so staff and the customer always see the same balance.

Linking Only on a Clear Match

A returning customer's sign-in links to their Pike13 history only when exactly one record matches their verified email. Anything less certain goes to staff, who record who decided and why. Account recovery requires proof that the customer owns the email address.

Reviewed Releases Only

Each database migration is approved by checksum before it applies, so only the reviewed version runs. There were 98 by November 24, 2026. That day the unit suite had 695 tests and none failed.

The migration is done. Now I maintain the platform, add features, and make the flows that mix in-person and digital steps more efficient. I am also building a training mobile app for the institute, which is in progress.
