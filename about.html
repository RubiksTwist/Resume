About

Hi, I'm Alex.

I coordinate projects, keep the people on them informed, and analyze the data behind decisions. I build data products too, and identity and security are the topics I enjoy most.

The work I like starts with learning how people do their jobs and ends with those jobs being easier. So far that has meant coordinating teams and vendors, writing the documentation people work from, and analyzing records to find what is wrong in them.

Right now I work on the operations platform I built for The Dog Institute, a Brooklyn dog-training business. I worked with the founder and the five-person team to map how they look up customers, sell packages, and take bookings, then built the customer portal and staff tools around those routines, with analog backups alongside the digital flows. Building it meant migrating nine years of customer history, moving payments to Square, and retiring Pike13 in November 2026. The migration consolidated 239,000+ source records into 2,180 customer records. Card processing cost 14.6% of sales in September 2026 on the old processor, and runs about 3% on Square. Now I maintain the platform, add features, and make the flows that mix in-person and digital steps more efficient. I am also building a training mobile app for the institute.

Before that I was an operations coordinator on a face-authentication testing program for Google, through Coforge. I ran day-to-day operations, kept the test devices ready, and was the point of contact for quality control, project managers, vendors, and facilities. With Handshake AI I researched real vulnerabilities to test whether AI models refuse to hand out working exploits, and turned the findings into short write-ups for reviewers. At Universal Music Group I updated records for 600+ applications in ServiceNow, wrote CyberArk documentation that covers about half of the team's elevated-access ticket patterns, and presented workflow findings to 300+ staff, including senior leadership.

I came up through networking and security. I earned a B.S. in Network Engineering and Security and then an M.S. in Cybersecurity and Information Assurance from Western Governors University, and built home labs along the way to see the attacks and defenses for myself. Identity and security are still the topics I most enjoy thinking in.

Outside of work I build data products. Each one starts with public data that is hard to use and ends with something people can search, with the source one click away. You can see them on the work page. My team also placed second in the Pros V Joes CTF at BSides NYC 2025.
